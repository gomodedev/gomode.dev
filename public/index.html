Choose where the conversation runs

Your voice. Your choice.

Choose the models, the language, and where each voice stage runs. The voice gateway runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux, serving web and Android clients.

All-in-one

Stream microphone audio to your gateway over WebRTC. One realtime model listens, thinks, and speaks, in the speaking voice you pick.

Separate stages

Pick recognition, a language model, and speech independently. Run each speech stage on the gateway, with cloud or local models, or on Android.

A model on your phone

Download Gemma 4 or LFM3 from Settings and run conversation turns on the device. The experimental Whistle + Needle engine matches speech to tool actions.

On-device voice is not an offline guarantee: speech availability depends on Android and its speech service, and host tools still need a connection. One voice language applies to recognition, speech, and replies.
