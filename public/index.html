Choose where the conversation runs

Your voice. Your choice.

Choose the speech mode, model, and language that fit your setup.

Cloud voice

Stream microphone audio to your gateway over WebRTC. Pick from its advertised audio choices and, where supported, choose the speaking voice you hear.

Device speech, remote model

Android handles speech recognition and reads replies aloud. A text-capable gateway handles the conversation and requests your host’s tools.

A model on your phone

Run conversation turns locally with Gemma 4 or LFM weights installed on the device. Experimental NeuDecide and Whistle + Needle engines match speech to tool actions.

On-device voice is not an offline guarantee: speech availability depends on Android and its speech service, and host tools still need a connection.
